Changes by Hanzi: selected character/word recordings by pinyin; reused isolated
homophones where the complete syllable and tone match; converted original FLAC
to 24 kHz mono 64 kbps MP3; normalized peak volume; added 60 ms leading and
100 ms trailing padding. No speech was cut out of words, no syllables were
stitched together, and no speed or pitch changes were applied. Recordings retain
their respective licenses above, separately from this app's MIT code license.

Per-file original archive entry, speaker, reading, license, SHA-256 checksums,
and homophone substitutions: /audio/v2/manifest.json
Import script and editable source: https://github.com/freeappstore-online/hanzi-practice
