Word examples (web/src/examples.json)

The examples combine curated words, short phrases and English glosses with
readings checked and corrected using CC-CEDICT, by the CC-CEDICT contributors.
Source: https://www.mdbg.net/chinese/dictionary?page=cc-cedict
Dataset consulted: 2026-11-02 release.
Changes: selected common beginner examples, short English glosses, marked-pinyin
formatting, spoken tone changes for yi/bu, and links to illustrative scenes.
